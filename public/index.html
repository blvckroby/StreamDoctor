One glance

A traffic light for every stream.

No percentages and no noisy diagnostics in the title. Status first, then quality, language and provider.

Ready

Direct and reachable. These are shown first.

Extra pipeline

Sidecar, extractor, sync or another processing step may be involved.

Problem detected

Timeout, HTTP error or another issue worth knowing before playback.

Dead

Confirmed unavailable links such as 404 or 410 responses.

Built as middleware

Your addons stay. StreamDoctor sorts the noise.

Add your upstream manifests once. StreamDoctor merges them, probes what can be checked and keeps the original stream data intact.

01 · STATUS

Adaptive probing

Fast check first, then a longer retry before a stream is considered slow. Host concurrency is limited to reduce false timeouts.

02 · MEDIA

Quality & language

Reads HLS metadata when available and falls back to provider labels without confusing subtitle language with spoken audio.

03 · ORDER

Useful ranking

Green, yellow, orange, red — then 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p and SD. Providers remain fairly interleaved.

Minimal output

Only what matters in the stream list.
